My work with Prof. Sudarshan Ananth focuses on the light-cone formulation of gauge theory, gravity, and $\mathcal{N}=8$ supergravity. I am interested in how working directly with physical field degrees of freedom helps us understand interactions in gauge theory and gravity.

## What I have been studying

I began with special relativity, index notation, the Lagrangian formulation of electromagnetism, the Lorentz group, and introductory path integrals. I then studied how the light-cone approach can be used to construct cubic interactions for fields of arbitrary spin using closure of the Poincare Algebra.

For gravity, I studied the gauge conditions and constraint equations used to reduce the Einstein–Hilbert action to the two physical graviton helicities in four dimensions. I explicitly calculated its cubic and quartic terms, comparing the original light-front treatment with later expressions from different reference sources.

## Currently working on

I am currently working on color–kinematics (CK) duality with Prof. Sudarshan Ananth. My current references are the following papers by Bern, Carrasco and Johansson (BCJ):

- Reference 14: [*New Relations for Gauge-Theory Amplitudes* (2008)](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.3993).
- Reference 15: [*Perturbative Quantum Gravity as a Double Copy of Gauge Theory* (2010)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.0476).

I am also studying $\mathcal{N}=4$ Yang–Mills theory and $\mathcal{N}=8$ supergravity in light-cone gauge. For supergravity, I am interested in its symmetries and how its interaction terms relate to pure gravity.

- Reference 16: Brink, Lindgren and Nilsson, *$\mathcal{N}=4$ Yang–Mills Theory on the Light Cone*.
- Reference 17: Ananth, Brink, Heise and Svendsen, [*The $\mathcal{N}=8$ Supergravity Hamiltonian as a Quadratic Form*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0607019).
