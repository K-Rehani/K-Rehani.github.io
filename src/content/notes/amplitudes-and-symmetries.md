These references sit alongside my light-cone study and point toward questions I would like to understand more deeply.

- Ananth and Theisen, [*KLT Relations from the Einstein–Hilbert Lagrangian*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.1778), studies how the three- and four-point gauge–gravity relations can be made visible at the level of a suitably rewritten action.
- Ananth, Brink, Heise and Svendsen, [*The N=8 Supergravity Hamiltonian as a Quadratic Form*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0607019), develops the light-cone supergravity Hamiltonian and its quartic interaction.
- Ananth, Brink, Majumdar, Mali and Shah, [*Gravitation and Quadratic Forms*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.06261), studies quadratic-form Hamiltonians and residual transformations in gravity.

These are reading references, not my publications. My own derivations and study notes will be added separately.
