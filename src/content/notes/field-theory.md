## Foundational reading

- Mark Srednicki, *Quantum Field Theory*, Chapters 1, 2, and 33: relativistic fields and the Lorentz group.
- A. Zee, *Quantum Field Theory in a Nutshell*, Sections I.1–I.3: the motivation for field theory and the path-integral approach.
- A. K. H. Bengtsson, I. Bengtsson and L. Brink, *Cubic Interaction Terms for Arbitrary Spin*: constructing interactions in the light-cone formulation.

## Current reading

Brink, Lindgren and Nilsson’s *N=4 Yang–Mills Theory on the Light Cone* develops a superspace description of maximally supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory. I am working through this as part of my current theoretical study.

Sarthak Parikh’s thesis, *Applications of Light-cone Superspace in Quantum Field Theory*, is an additional reference.
