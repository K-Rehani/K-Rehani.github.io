These topics formed the starting point of my reading with Prof. Sudarshan Ananth.

## Topics covered

- Special relativity and relativistic electrodynamics, following Chapter 12 of Griffiths’ *Introduction to Electrodynamics*.
- Vectors, index notation, coordinate transformations, and contractions.
- The least-action principle, relativistic invariance, and the electromagnetic field Lagrangian.

## Conventions

For my own write-ups, I use the mostly-plus spacetime metric,

$$
\eta_{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,+1,+1,+1).
$$

A reference may use a different convention; signs and raised or lowered indices should be checked before combining formulas from different sources.
