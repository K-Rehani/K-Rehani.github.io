My gravity reading follows the reduction of the Einstein–Hilbert action to the two physical graviton helicities in four dimensions, and the expansion of the action in powers of the fields.

## Main reading

- J. Scherk and J. H. Schwarz, *Gravitation in the Light Cone Gauge* — studied in part.
- I. Bengtsson, M. Cederwall and O. Lindgren, *Light Front Actions for Gravity and Higher Spins; Some Further Results*.
- The quartic-contribution working sheet supplied during the project.
- Appendix C of Ananth, Brink, Heise and Svendsen, [*The N=8 Supergravity Hamiltonian as a Quadratic Form*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0607019), as a reference for the pure-gravity action.

## Further references

- Goroff and Schwarz, *D-Dimensional Gravity in the Light-cone Gauge*.
- Mahendraprasad Mali, *Aspects of Gravitation in the Light-cone Gauge* (PhD thesis, 2016).
- Sucheta Majumdar, *Symmetries in Gravity and Supergravity in the Light-cone Gauge* (PhD thesis, 2018).
- Ananth, [*The Quintic Interaction Vertex in Light-cone Gravity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.1494).
- Ananth, Bhave and Raj, [*The Structure of Interaction Vertices in Pure Gravity in the Light-cone Gauge*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.10416).

The last two papers are references for higher-order interactions. Listing a reference here does not mean that I have completed all of its calculations.
